Before booking

What driver details do I need?

Use a sample name and email to try Detour. For a live rental, check the operator’s minimum driver age, licence and ID requirements, and rules for additional drivers.

How much will the rental cost?

Choose your dates and a car to see the rental subtotal, estimated taxes and fees, and demo total. If you change the dates in My booking, you can review the new estimate before saving.

Is there a deposit or card hold?

The Detour demo uses a sample total and collects zero payment. For a live rental, check the deposit amount, accepted cards, and when any hold will be released.

Mileage and fuel

How much mileage is included?

Mileage is an operator policy. For a live rental, check the included distance, any charge for extra miles, and whether your route crosses a restricted area.

Do I need to refuel before returning?

Check the operator’s required return fuel level and refuelling charges. Add a fuel stop to your itinerary if your rental terms require one.

Pick-up and changes

Where do I pick up and return the car?

Choose Portland Downtown or Portland Airport in the demo. Your confirmation shows the selected branch and opens its pick-up guide. Both branches are illustrative.

Can I change or cancel my reservation?

Open My booking to edit the dates or pick-up location, review the updated estimate, or cancel your demo reservation. Live cancellation fees and deadlines depend on the operator.
